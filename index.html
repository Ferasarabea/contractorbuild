08 / QUESTIONS

What working with
Contractor Build means.

We are not selling a single tactic. We build the marketing infrastructure around the way your contracting business gets found, receives leads, follows up, and wins work.

What kind of company is Contractor Build?

Contractor Build is a digital marketing and growth partner focused on contractors and local home-service companies. We combine website development, search marketing, paid advertising, local visibility, tracking, reputation, and automation so the pieces support one another.

Do I have to buy every service?

No. The right scope depends on what is already working, where leads are being lost, your market, budget, capacity, and growth goals. An engagement can begin with one priority—such as a website, SEO, or paid acquisition—and expand when the data supports it.

How do you decide what to work on first?

We start by auditing the customer journey: visibility, website conversion, advertising, local presence, tracking, follow-up, reviews, and competitors. Then we prioritize the bottlenecks most likely to affect qualified lead flow and measurement.

How is performance measured?

Traffic and rankings provide context, but they are not the whole scoreboard. Where the available systems allow it, we connect marketing activity to calls, forms, qualified leads, estimates, appointments, and booked outcomes so decisions are closer to revenue.

Which contractors do you work with?

Our strategy is designed for local service and trade businesses including HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, locksmith, remodeling, landscaping, painting, garage door, moving, cleaning, restoration, concrete, flooring, pest control, pools, fencing, solar, and related contractor categories.

What happens after the free marketing audit?

We identify gaps and opportunities across your current marketing, explain which issues matter most, and outline a practical growth plan. If Contractor Build is a fit to implement it, we can discuss scope and next steps; the audit itself is designed to give you a clearer picture of the system.
