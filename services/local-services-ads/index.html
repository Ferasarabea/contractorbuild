What this service solves

Operate eligible LSA campaigns with accurate services, areas, budgets, and lead workflows. Contractor Build approaches the work as part of the complete customer-acquisition system, so the message, traffic source, conversion experience, tracking, and follow-up support the same business goal.

  • Incorrect services or areas create irrelevant leads.
  • Slow lead handling reduces the value of expensive inquiries.
  • Teams lack a consistent review and dispute workflow.

What is included

  • Eligibility and profile-readiness review
  • Service and service-area configuration
  • Budget and schedule planning
  • Lead-status and available dispute workflows
  • Performance and lead-quality monitoring

Our contractor-first process

We confirm accurate business information, configure only real services and areas, document response ownership, review lead outcomes, and adjust budgets from observed capacity and quality. Google controls screening, badges, and placement.

How this looks in practice

Locksmith and plumbing inquiries are time-sensitive; routing and answer speed often matter as much as campaign settings.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly should this work produce results?

Timing depends on the starting point, market, channel, budget, and sales process. We set leading indicators and review qualified opportunities rather than promising a fixed result.

How will performance be measured?

Measurement is agreed before launch and can include qualified calls, forms, appointments, estimates, pipeline value, and sold work when CRM data is available.

Can this work with our existing website and systems?

Often, yes. We audit what is already useful, identify integration constraints, and recommend replacement only when the evidence supports it.
