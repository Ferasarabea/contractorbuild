Contractor Competitor Research

Understand the local choices customers see before deciding where to compete.

What this service solves

Understand the local choices customers see before deciding where to compete. Contractor Build approaches the work as part of the complete customer-acquisition system, so the message, traffic source, conversion experience, tracking, and follow-up support the same business goal.

  • Strategy copies the loudest competitor.
  • National brands are compared with unrelated local operators.
  • Research stops at keyword rankings.

What is included

  • Real search-result and market set
  • Service, offer, and message comparison
  • Organic, Maps, paid, and review observations
  • Opportunity and risk brief

Our contractor-first process

We define true competitors by service and geography, document what customers encounter, distinguish evidence from assumptions, and turn gaps into prioritized tests—not imitation.

How this looks in practice

A fencing company may discover competitors ignore commercial work, while a roofer may find estimate speed and financing dominate paid messages.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly should this work produce results?

Timing depends on the starting point, market, channel, budget, and sales process. We set leading indicators and review qualified opportunities rather than promising a fixed result.

How will performance be measured?

Measurement is agreed before launch and can include qualified calls, forms, appointments, estimates, pipeline value, and sold work when CRM data is available.

Can this work with our existing website and systems?

Often, yes. We audit what is already useful, identify integration constraints, and recommend replacement only when the evidence supports it.
