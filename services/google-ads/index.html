Google Ads Management

Capture high-intent searches with disciplined targeting and conversion measurement.

What this service solves

Capture high-intent searches with disciplined targeting and conversion measurement. Contractor Build approaches the work as part of the complete customer-acquisition system, so the message, traffic source, conversion experience, tracking, and follow-up support the same business goal.

  • Broad queries spend budget outside profitable services.
  • Ads send every visitor to a generic homepage.
  • Reported conversions include spam, wrong numbers, and unqualified calls.

What is included

  • Search-term and keyword strategy
  • Negative keywords and geographic controls
  • Service-specific ads and landing pages
  • Call, form, and qualified-lead tracking
  • Budget pacing, experiments, and plain-language reporting

Our contractor-first process

We define eligible services and geography, build tightly related campaigns, connect each intent to a relevant page, verify conversion signals, and optimize using lead quality—not clicks alone.

How this looks in practice

An electrician can separate emergency troubleshooting from panels, generators, and EV chargers so budgets and messages reflect different values and urgency.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly should this work produce results?

Timing depends on the starting point, market, channel, budget, and sales process. We set leading indicators and review qualified opportunities rather than promising a fixed result.

How will performance be measured?

Measurement is agreed before launch and can include qualified calls, forms, appointments, estimates, pipeline value, and sold work when CRM data is available.

Can this work with our existing website and systems?

Often, yes. We audit what is already useful, identify integration constraints, and recommend replacement only when the evidence supports it.
