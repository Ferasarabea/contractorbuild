Contractor Lead Generation

Build a balanced acquisition system around qualified opportunities—not list purchases.

What this service solves

Build a balanced acquisition system around qualified opportunities—not list purchases. Contractor Build approaches the work as part of the complete customer-acquisition system, so the message, traffic source, conversion experience, tracking, and follow-up support the same business goal.

  • Lead sources are unpredictable or impossible to attribute.
  • Volume overwhelms the team while quality remains unclear.
  • Marketing and sales use different definitions of a qualified lead.

What is included

  • Channel and offer strategy
  • Search, local, and paid-social acquisition
  • Conversion pages and lead qualification
  • Source, call, form, and pipeline tracking
  • Capacity-aware budget and follow-up planning

Our contractor-first process

We define a qualified opportunity, select channels around intent and sales cycle, build conversion paths, connect tracking to the pipeline, and reallocate effort using real outcomes.

How this looks in practice

A high-ticket remodeler may prefer fewer well-qualified consultations, while an emergency plumber needs immediate calls within a tight dispatch radius.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly should this work produce results?

Timing depends on the starting point, market, channel, budget, and sales process. We set leading indicators and review qualified opportunities rather than promising a fixed result.

How will performance be measured?

Measurement is agreed before launch and can include qualified calls, forms, appointments, estimates, pipeline value, and sold work when CRM data is available.

Can this work with our existing website and systems?

Often, yes. We audit what is already useful, identify integration constraints, and recommend replacement only when the evidence supports it.
